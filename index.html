Hello! Let’s get to know Claude together

Claude is a friendly helper on your phone or computer. You ask in your own words, by typing or talking, and it answers in plain English. Press Play and watch how it works. It takes about a minute.

Tip: you can stop at any step and press Next when you’re ready. Nothing moves until you say so.

It all comes down to three steps

That’s really it. You can repeat the last step as often as you like.

1

Open Claude

Tap the Claude app on your phone, or open it on your computer. “New chat” gives you a fresh page.

2

Ask your way

Type in the box at the bottom, or tap the microphone and simply talk.

3

Read and chat back

Not quite what you wanted? Say so, like you would to a friend. It happily tries again.

Six handy phrases worth keeping nearby

Say these any time, in the middle of any chat.

    Good to know

    It’s smart, not perfect

    Claude sometimes gets things wrong. For health, money or legal matters, check with your doctor, pharmacist, bank or lawyer.

    Keep private things private

    There’s no need to type passwords, card numbers or your Social Security number.

    Claude never calls you

    It won’t phone, email, or ask for money or gift cards. If someone does, it isn’t Claude.

    You can’t break it

    Pressed the wrong thing? No harm done. Tap “New chat” and start fresh.

    Take your time. There’s no rush, and no question is too small.
